Privacy Policy

Your screen stays on your Mac.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

HingeFlow does not collect, store, analyze, sell, or transmit personal data.

Screen content is processed only in memory on your Mac to render the visual effect. It is not recorded, saved, or uploaded. HingeFlow contains no analytics, advertising, accounts, or third-party tracking SDKs and makes no network requests.

HingeFlow reads the MacBook's built-in lid-angle sensor only to control the on-device visual effect.
